## Focus

Risk rule 4 fired: the task adds a dependency that is not on the allowlist, has an unknown
or copyleft license, ships install scripts, or is very new or little used. Judge from the
engine's package facts, never from memory, whether the dependency is the intended package,
needed, maintained and acceptable; cite the fact rows.
<!-- vtc:editable -->

## Heuristics

- When the task needs only one function of a large package, say so.
- A package name one character away from a popular one needs its publisher checked.
